# Research Feedback: KJA Rejection - GiEVs & Lung Injury

**Manuscript**: Ginger-derived extracellular vesicles attenuate aspiration-induced lung injury in mice: involvement of miR-339-3p (KJA26305)
**Date**: 2026-04-29
**Status**: Rejected (Pre-external review)

## 🔴 Key Reviewer Concerns
1. **Novelty**: Many similar papers on miR-339-3p exist; anti-inflammatory effects not well-established/novel enough for KJA.
2. **Clinical Relevance (Survival Rate)**: Survival data is missing for the 48h monitoring period. This is considered crucial for clinical relevance.
3. **Long-term Follow-up**: Reviewer requested long-term data for:
   - FEV0.1 recovery (Fig 2B)
   - Lung wet-to-dry ratio (Fig 3A)
   - WBC count in BALF (Fig 3B) - WBC remains high (2 K/μL vs normal 0.1-0.2 K/μL).

## 💡 Strategic Actions
- [ ] **Data Re-analysis**: Check if survival data was recorded for the 18 mice per group.
- [ ] **Literature Review**: Search for papers specifically linking miR-339-3p to anti-inflammatory mechanisms to strengthen the novelty argument.
- [ ] **Journal Selection**: Identify journals that prioritize mechanistic studies over strict clinical anesthesia relevance (e.g., *Pharmaceutical Science Advances* or *European Journal of Inflammation* - both recently contacted via email).
- [ ] **Novelty Refinement**: Re-frame the story to emphasize the "Ginger-derived" delivery aspect if miR-339-3p itself is not the primary novelty.
