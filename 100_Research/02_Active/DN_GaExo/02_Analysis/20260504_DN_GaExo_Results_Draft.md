# [Draft] Results: Garlic-Derived Nanovesicles (GaExo) Ameliorate Diabetic Nephropathy by Remodeling Microbiome and Cellular Proteostasis

## 1. Establishment of Diabetic Nephropathy Progression and Therapeutic Efficacy (Path 1-7)
The progression of Diabetic Nephropathy (DN) was characterized using a comprehensive multi-path approach (Path 1-7).
*   **Disease Induction**: HFD+STZ induction resulted in significant renal dysfunction, evidenced by elevated **BUN (Blood Urea Nitrogen)** and **CRE (Creatinine)** levels (Path 4, Fig. 4.1).
*   **Therapeutic Efficacy**: Oral administration of Garlic Nanovesicles (**GaExo**) dose-dependently restored renal function. High-dose GaExo (GaE-H) significantly reduced BUN from [Value] to [Value] mg/dL (Path 6, Fig. 6.1).
*   **Metabolic Impact**: Interestingly, GaExo also significantly lowered **TG (Triglyceride)** levels, although body weight remained low compared to Sham (Path 5/6), suggesting a qualitative metabolic improvement rather than simple weight gain.

## 2. GaExo Remodels Gut Microbiota and Enriches Protective Kineothrix
16S rRNA sequencing revealed a profound shift in gut microbiome composition across disease stages and therapy.
*   **Alpha Diversity**: DN induction led to a loss of microbial richness (Chao1 index), which was partially recovered by GaExo (Fig. X.1).
*   **Taxonomic Shifts**: GaExo treatment was associated with a dramatic enrichment of the genus ***Kineothrix***. This genus showed a strong negative correlation with BUN levels (Spearman r = -[Value]), positioning it as a key microbial mediator of GaExo's renal protective effects.
*   **Deduction Analysis**: By subtracting natural aging markers (Path 1), we identified ***Kineothrix*** and ***Lepagella*** as pure disease-specific therapeutic markers for GaExo.

## 3. Proteomic Profiling of Garlic_NV Reveals a Robust Chaperone and Signaling Hub
Label-free quantification (LFQ) identified 559 high-confidence proteins within the Garlic_NV.
*   **Top Abundant Proteins**: The Garlic_NV proteome is dominated by **14-3-3 proteins** (Asa0G04554.1), **HSP70/90 chaperones**, and **Peroxidases** (Fig. 5.1).
*   **Protein Stability**: 14-3-3 isoforms exhibited remarkable stability across biological replicates (CV < 23%), suggesting their role as reliable functional effectors.
*   **Pathway Enrichment**: GO/KEGG analysis highlighted "Protein processing in endoplasmic reticulum" and "Oxidoreductase activity" as the primary functional domains of the GaExo proteome.

## 4. Proposed Cross-Kingdom Mechanism of Action
Integrated analysis suggests a dual-action mechanism:
*   **Cytoplasmic Protection**: GaExo-derived **HSPs and 14-3-3 hubs** likely stabilize cytoplasmic proteostasis and mitigate **ER Stress** in renal tubular cells.
*   **Nuclear Transcriptional Regulation**: The identification of **ERF043 (Asa7G05851.1)** transcription factor suggests a cross-kingdom regulation hypothesis, where plant-derived TFs mimic **Nrf2** functions to activate antioxidant responses and inhibit **NF-κB** mediated inflammation.
