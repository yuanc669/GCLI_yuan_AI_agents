# [Plan] Mechanism Validation Protocol for DN_GaExo Project

## 1. Objective
To experimentally validate the multi-omics derived hypotheses regarding Garlic_NV (GaExo) therapeutic mechanisms in the kidney tissue of DN mice.

## 2. Validation Module A: ER Stress & Proteostasis (GaExo HSPs/14-3-3 Target)
*   **Hypothesis**: GaExo-delivered HSPs and 14-3-3 proteins mitigate diabetes-induced ER stress and apoptosis in renal tubular cells.
*   **Assay**: Western Blot (WB) & Immunohistochemistry (IHC)
*   **Targets**:
    *   **GRP78 (BiP)**: Master regulator of ER stress (Expected: DN ⬆️, GaExo ⬇️).
    *   **CHOP (GADD153)**: Pro-apoptotic ER stress marker (Expected: DN ⬆️, GaExo ⬇️).
    *   **14-3-3 Zeta/Delta**: To confirm the presence of high-abundance 14-3-3 in kidney tissue after oral administration (Expected: GaExo ⬆️).
    *   **Cleaved Caspase-3**: Apoptosis marker (Expected: DN ⬆️, GaExo ⬇️).

## 3. Validation Module B: Antioxidant & Anti-inflammation (ERF043/Peroxidase Target)
*   **Hypothesis**: GaExo activates the Nrf2 antioxidant pathway and inhibits NF-κB inflammatory signaling, potentially mediated by ERF043 cross-kingdom regulation.
*   **Assay**: Western Blot (Nuclear/Cytosolic Fractionation) & IHC
*   **Targets**:
    *   **Nrf2 (Nuclear)**: Master antioxidant transcription factor (Expected: GaExo ⬆️ nuclear translocation).
    *   **HO-1 (Heme Oxygenase-1)**: Downstream antioxidant enzyme (Expected: GaExo ⬆️).
    *   **Phospho-NF-κB p65**: Active inflammatory signaling (Expected: DN ⬆️, GaExo ⬇️).
    *   **8-OHdG**: Oxidative DNA damage marker (IHC only, Expected: DN ⬆️, GaExo ⬇️).

## 4. Sample Requirements
*   **Tissue**: Fresh frozen kidney cortex (for WB) and 4% PFA-fixed paraffin-embedded kidney sections (for IHC).
*   **Groups**: Sham, DN (Untreated), DN + GaExo (Low dose), DN + GaExo (High dose).
