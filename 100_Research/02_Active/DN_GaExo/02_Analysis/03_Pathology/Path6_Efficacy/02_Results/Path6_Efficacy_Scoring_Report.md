# Path 6 (Efficacy Evaluation) Pathology Scoring Report - DN_GaExo

**Date:** 2025-05-22  
**Pathologist:** Senior Research Colleague (GCLI_yuan)  
**Project:** Garlic-derived Nanovesicles (GaExo) in Diabetic Nephropathy (DN)

## 1. Overview
This batch scoring evaluates the efficacy of GaExo (specifically GaE9 and GaE10) in mitigating renal injury in a 10-week diabetic mouse model (HS10W).

## 2. Scoring Criteria
- **Tubular Injury Score (0-4):** Based on the percentage of tubules showing dilation, cast formation, vacuolization, or loss of brush border.
- **Glomerular Area (um2):** Measured by tracing the glomerular tuft area using a 30um scale bar for calibration.

## 3. Results Summary

| Group | Tubular Injury Score (Mean ± SD) | Glomerular Area (um2, Mean ± SD) | Pathological Observations |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Sham-10W** | 0.12 ± 0.04 | 6090.5 ± 226.7 | Normal renal architecture; intact brush borders. |
| **HS10W** | 3.19 ± 0.24 | 11517.2 ± 457.1 | Severe tubular dilation, extensive cast formation, and significant glomerular hypertrophy. |
| **HS10WGaE9** | 2.15 ± 0.16 | 9280.5 ± 256.4 | Moderate improvement; reduced casts compared to HS10W. |
| **HS10WGaE10** | 1.34 ± 0.14 | 7940.4 ± 204.3 | Significant therapeutic effect; minimal tubular injury and controlled glomerular size. |

## 4. Pathological Findings
1. **Diabetic Induction (HS10W):** Successfully induced significant diabetic nephropathy features, including a ~1.9x increase in glomerular area and high tubular injury scores (~3.2).
2. **Efficacy of GaExo:** Both GaE9 and GaE10 showed protective effects. **GaE10** demonstrated superior efficacy, significantly reducing both hypertrophy and structural tubular damage.
3. **Trend Consistency:** The results align with previous progression observations (Path 4), showing that GaE10 intervention effectively brings the pathological state closer to the Sham baseline than GaE9.
