# 摘要報告：Sham 健康基準線綜合分析
# Summary Report: Sham Healthy Baseline Integrated Analysis

**日期 (Date)**: 2026-05-01
**專案 (Project)**: DN_GaExo

---

## 📊 概覽 (Overview)
本報告彙整了健康對照組在 10 週觀察期內的生理與腸道菌叢變動，旨在建立 GaExo 治療效果評估的「健康金標準」。
This report aggregates the physiological and microbial changes in the healthy control group over a 10-week period, aiming to establish a "Healthy Gold Standard" for evaluating GaExo treatment efficacy.

---

## 📈 生理代謝動態 (Physiological & Metabolic Dynamics)
- **體重增長 (Body Weight)**: 動物展現穩定的生理成長。
  - W0: 19.78 ± 0.3g → W10: 26.66 ± 0.5g (↑)。
- **生化基準 (Biochemistry)**: 
  - 腎功能 (BUN) 與 脂質代謝 (TG/CHOL) 均維持在健康成年水平。
  - Kidney function (BUN) and lipid metabolism (TG/CHOL) maintained healthy adult levels.

---

## 🧬 腸道菌叢成熟特徵 (Microbial Maturation Features)

### 1. 優勢菌種自然演替 (Dominant Species Succession)
- ***Bacteroides acidifaciens***: 豐度顯著下降 (Significant decrease)。
  - W0: 15.7% → W10: 7.2%。這反映了宿主由幼齡向成年期轉化時的菌相自然成熟。
  - Reflects natural maturation from juvenile to adult stages.

### 2. 功能菌群穩定性 (Functional Stability)
- ***Kineothrix sp000403275***: 展現極高的穩定性 (Highly stable)。
  - W0: 1.7% → W10: 2.4%。
  - **科研意義**: 該菌在健康狀態下不會隨時間減少，因此其在疾病組中的流失具備病理特異性。
  - **Significance**: Stable Kineothrix in health confirms its loss in disease is pathologically specific, not time-dependent.

## 💡 核心結論 (Core Conclusions)
1. **基準線價值 (Baseline Value)**: 本報告確立了「健康成年菌相」的樣貌，為評估 GaExo 能否將 HS 組推回「健康穩態」提供了比較基準。
Establishes the "Healthy Adult Microbiota" profile, providing a benchmark to evaluate GaExo's ability to restore homeostasis.
2. **排除假陽性 (Excluding False Positives)**: 區分了「隨時間自然變動」與「因疾病導致變動」的菌種，提高了生物標記的準確性。
Differentiates natural time-dependent shifts from disease-induced changes, improving biomarker accuracy.
