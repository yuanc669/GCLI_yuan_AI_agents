**日期 (Date)**: 2026-05-01

---

## 📊 概覽 (Overview)
本報告彙整了 Sham（對照組）與 Sham10W（10 週進程組）的腸道菌叢比較結果。
This report aggregates the results comparing the Sham (Control) and Sham10W (10-week progression) groups.

---

## 📈 Alpha 多樣性 (Alpha Diversity)
- **Shannon 指數 (Shannon Index)**: 維持穩定 (Maintained)。
  - Sham: 3.58 ± 0.15; Sham10W: 3.73 ± 0.06.
- **觀察物種數 (Observed Species/Richness)**: **顯著下降 (Significant decrease)** 在 Sham10W 組。
  - Sham: 158.7 ± 9.3; Sham10W: 120.7 ± 4.0 (p < 0.05).
- **解讀 (Interpretation)**: 10 週的疾病模型進程導致菌種豐富度顯著下降，顯示菌相生態系的穩定性受損。
The 10-week progression model shows a significant loss of microbial richness, suggesting impaired ecosystem stability.

## 💡 關鍵結論 (Key Conclusions)
1. **腸道菌相失衡 (Intestinal Dysbiosis)**: Sham10W 模型呈現顯著失衡，特別是核心共生菌 *B. acidifaciens* 的流失與 *B. thetaiotaomicron* 的異常增生。
The Sham10W model exhibits marked dysbiosis, characterized by the loss of core commensal B. acidifaciens and proliferation of B. thetaiotaomicron.
2. **致病潛力 (Pathogenic Potential)**: *Mucispirillum schaedleri* 與 *Suilimivivens* 的增加可能與 DN 進程中的發炎或代謝紊亂有關。
Increase in Mucispirillum schaedleri and Suilimivivens may be linked to inflammatory or metabolic dysregulation during DN progression.
3. **生態複雜度降低 (Reduced Complexity)**: 觀察物種數的顯著減少預示著模型中菌相生態系的脆弱性。
The significant reduction in observed species richness indicates a more fragile microbial ecosystem in this model.
