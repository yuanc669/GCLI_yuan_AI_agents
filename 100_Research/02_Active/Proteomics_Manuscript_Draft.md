## 1. Materials and Methods: Proteomic Profiling of GiEVs

### Proteomic Analysis via LC-MS/MS
To characterize the protein composition of ginger-derived extracellular vesicles (GiEVs), label-free quantitative proteomics was performed. Briefly, GiEV proteins were extracted using RIPA buffer, followed by protein concentration determination using a BCA assay. Protein samples (20 μg) were subjected to in-gel trypsin digestion. The resulting peptides were analyzed using an LTQ Orbitrap XL mass spectrometer (Thermo Fisher Scientific) coupled with an Agilent 1200 HPLC system.

Mass spectra were searched against the *Zingiber officinale* protein database using the Sequest HT search engine within Proteome Discoverer software (version 2.4, Thermo Fisher Scientific). Peptide identification required a high-confidence filter (FDR < 1%). Protein abundance was calculated based on precursor ion intensity. Functional annotation and pathway analysis were conducted using Gene Ontology (GO) and KEGG databases to identify anti-inflammatory and antioxidant-related protein clusters.
